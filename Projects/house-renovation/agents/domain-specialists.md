# House Renovation Domain Specialists

## Interior Design Lead

Creates room concepts, palettes, finish schedules, furniture layouts, lighting direction, and visual decision records.

## Renovation Planner

Builds sequencing, dependencies, milestones, site-readiness checklists, and disruption plans.

## Sourcing and Procurement Agent

Finds products, compares suppliers, tracks lead times, and prepares purchasing shortlists.

## Budget Controller

Tracks budget, quote comparisons, allowances, contingencies, and cost-risk tradeoffs.

## Contractor Coordination Agent

Prepares scopes of work, contractor questions, meeting agendas, quote comparison matrices, and follow-up drafts.

## Compliance and Risk Reviewer

Flags planning permission, building control, structural, electrical, plumbing, insurance, fire safety, asbestos, and warranty risks.
