# Contractor Quote Review Workflow

## Inputs

- Quote document.
- Scope of work.
- Timeline.
- Assumptions and exclusions.

## Review Checklist

- Scope coverage.
- Missing line items.
- Materials and labor split.
- Payment schedule.
- Timeline and dependencies.
- Warranty.
- Insurance.
- Change-order process.
- Regulatory responsibilities.

## Output

- Quote comparison summary.
- Risk list.
- Clarifying questions.
- Recommendation.
