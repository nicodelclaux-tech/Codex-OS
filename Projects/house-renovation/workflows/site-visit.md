# Site Visit Workflow

## Before Visit

- Prepare room checklist.
- List measurements needed.
- List photos needed.
- Confirm contractor or vendor questions.

## During Visit

- Capture photos.
- Capture measurements.
- Note defects, constraints, and surprises.
- Record decisions and open questions separately.

## After Visit

- Update project journal.
- Create learning entries for missed prep.
- Update room briefs.
- Route follow-ups to the right specialist.
