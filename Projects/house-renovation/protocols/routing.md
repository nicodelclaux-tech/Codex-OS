# House Renovation Routing

## Request Routing

- "What should this room look like?" -> Interior Design Lead.
- "What order should we do this in?" -> Renovation Planner.
- "Find products or compare options" -> Sourcing and Procurement Agent.
- "Can we afford this?" -> Budget Controller.
- "What do I ask the contractor?" -> Contractor Coordination Agent.
- "Is this risky or regulated?" -> Compliance and Risk Reviewer.

## Approval Required

- Sending contractor messages.
- Booking appointments.
- Purchasing products.
- Accepting quotes.
- Changing budget or scope.
- Any structural, electrical, plumbing, or compliance-sensitive recommendation.
