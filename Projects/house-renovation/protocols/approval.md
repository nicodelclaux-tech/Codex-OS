# House Renovation Approval Protocol

Approval is required before external or expensive action.

## Approval Gates

- Spend above agreed threshold.
- Contractor commitment.
- Design choice that affects multiple rooms.
- Structural or regulated work.
- Scheduling that affects other people.

## Required Decision Record

Before approval, create a decision note with:

- Options considered.
- Recommendation.
- Cost.
- Timeline impact.
- Risks.
- Reversibility.
